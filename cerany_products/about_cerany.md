# About CERANY
CERANY is an online K-beauty store based in Busan, South Korea. It sells Korean skincare products: cleansers, toners, serums, creams, sunscreens and masks.

CeraGPT is the AI skincare assistant of CERANY. It helps customers choose products for their skin type and explains how to build a Korean skincare routine.

Shipping: Free shipping in Korea for orders over 30,000 KRW.
Customer support: available Monday to Friday, 10:00-18:00 KST.
