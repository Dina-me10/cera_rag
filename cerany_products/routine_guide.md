# CERANY Skincare Routine Guide

## Morning routine (basic)
1. CERANY Low pH Gentle Gel Cleanser
2. CERANY Hyaluronic Water Toner
3. Serum (Niacinamide for oily skin, Cica for sensitive skin, Vitamin C for dull skin)
4. Moisturizer
5. Sunscreen: CERANY Airy Sun Fluid (oily) or Mild Mineral Sun Cream (sensitive/dry)

## Evening routine
1. CERANY Melting Cleansing Balm (if you wore sunscreen or makeup)
2. CERANY Low pH Gentle Gel Cleanser
3. Toner (BHA Pore Clearing Toner 2-3 times a week for oily skin)
4. Serum
5. Night cream: Ceramide Barrier Cream (dry) or Retinal Night Renewal Cream (anti-aging)

## Ingredient rules
- Do not use Vitamin C and Retinol at the same time: Vitamin C in the morning, retinol at night.
- Do not use BHA and Retinol on the same night.
- Always use sunscreen when using BHA, Vitamin C or Retinol.
- For severe acne or skin problems, see a dermatologist.
