# CERANY Ceramide Barrier Cream
Category: Moisturizer
Skin type: Dry, very dry, sensitive
Key ingredients: Ceramide NP, Panthenol, Shea Butter, Squalane
Price: 28,000 KRW (50 ml)

A rich cream that repairs the skin barrier and locks in moisture for 24 hours. Ceramides refill the gaps between skin cells, so water does not escape. Recommended for winter dryness, flaky patches and tight-feeling skin.

How to use: Apply as the last step of your evening routine. In very dry weather, also use in the morning before sunscreen.
Avoid if: You have very oily, acne-prone skin (texture may feel heavy).
